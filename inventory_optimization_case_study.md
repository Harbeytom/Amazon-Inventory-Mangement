# Inventory Optimization Case Study (Simulated)

## Scenario
A fictional seller manages a small catalog across Amazon FBA and FBM. The seller wants a repeatable way to review stock coverage and prioritize replenishment.

## Challenge
Inventory information is spread across separate lists, making it harder to spot products that may need attention before stock runs low.

## Demonstration Approach
1. Organize sample products by SKU, ASIN placeholder, fulfillment method, available units, and average daily sales.
2. Record supplier lead time, safety-stock assumptions, target coverage, and inbound units.
3. Calculate estimated days of cover and a sample reorder point.
4. Flag records for review when available plus inbound stock is at or below the illustrative reorder point.
5. Present the findings in a spreadsheet that can be reviewed and adapted.

## Illustrative Findings
The sample dataset includes both healthy-stock and low-stock scenarios. Products flagged for review should be checked against current Seller Central inventory, reserved units, inbound shipment status, sales trends, supplier lead times, and cash-flow constraints before action.

## Outcome
The demonstration provides a consistent format for inventory review and replenishment discussion. It does **not** represent measured client results or an actual Amazon account audit.

## Assumptions and Limitations
- All data is fictional.
- ASIN values are demo placeholders, not verified catalog identifiers.
- Sales rates and lead times are assumed for illustration.
- Reorder calculations are basic planning aids, not purchasing instructions.
- No live Amazon Seller Central connection or API integration is included.
